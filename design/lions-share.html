Kenya · updated 27 August 2026

Online casinos in Kenya

The short answer

Yes — online casino gambling is licensed in Kenya and actively policed. The Betting Control and Licensing Board licenses both casino and sports betting under the Gambling Control Act, which requires local ownership and levies 15% on gross gaming revenue.

The BCLB has suspended licences and blocked unlicensed operators, so the difference between licensed and unlicensed is real, not decorative.

Questions Kenyan players ask

Why has my M-Pesa withdrawal not arrived?
Four causes account for most of them. KYC not completed; withdrawing to a different M-Pesa number from the one you registered with; bonus funds still under a wagering requirement; or a large amount held for extra checks. If none apply and the operator is BCLB-licensed, the BCLB is who you escalate to.
Do I pay tax on winnings in Kenya?
Withholding tax applies to winnings and is deducted by the operator before payout — check the current rate, which has changed more than once in recent years.
How do I check a casino is really BCLB-licensed?
Find the licence number in the site footer, then look it up on the BCLB's own register — not on the casino's page. If the number is missing or does not appear, that is your answer.
