Common questions

The answers people actually search for, answered with the figures rather than around them.

Are online casinos in Africa legal?
It depends entirely on the country, and the differences are stark. Kenya, Ghana, Tanzania and Uganda all license online casinos properly. Nigeria regulates state by state following the Supreme Court ruling of November 2024. South Africa prohibits online casino outright under the National Gambling Act 2004, and Egypt not only bans it but began blocking sites in February 2026. There is no continent-wide answer — check your own country's page.
Can I play at international casino sites from Africa?
You usually can, but the licence is what matters, not the location. An operator licensed in your country answers to a regulator you can complain to. An offshore site with a Curaçao licence does not, and if it refuses a withdrawal your only route is a foreign regulator with no obligation to you. Where a local licence exists, use it.
How do I choose the best online casinos in Africa?
Find the licence number in the footer, then look it up on the regulator's own register. Not the badge image on the casino's page — the register. After that, check the withdrawal cap against the size of win you are hoping for, read the wagering requirement rather than the headline bonus, and confirm the payment method you use is supported for withdrawals as well as deposits.
What are the most popular casino games in Africa?
Slots, by a wide margin, then live dealer roulette and blackjack. Mobile play dominates almost everywhere, which is why the lighter apps tend to outperform the feature-heavy ones on African connections. Aviator-style crash games have taken a large share of the market in Kenya and Nigeria specifically.
Are African casinos safe to play at?
A locally licensed one is meaningfully safer than an offshore one, for a practical reason rather than a technical one. Encryption and game fairness are much the same everywhere. What differs is recourse: if a licensed Kenyan operator refuses to pay, the Gambling Regulatory Authority can act. If an offshore site does, nobody in Kenya can.
Do African slots have reasonable payout rates?
Return-to-player is set by the game supplier, not by the casino or your location. A Pragmatic Play or Evolution title runs the same RTP in Lagos as in London. What varies is whether the operator uses certified suppliers at all — which is one of the things a real licence is checking.
Are bonuses available at top online casinos in Africa?
Yes, and the headline figure is rarely the useful number. 1xBet Kenya advertises up to KES 26,000, at 5x wagering on odds of 1.40 or better — which is considerably harder to clear than SportPesa's KES 5,000. Read the wagering requirement, the expiry, and the maximum withdrawal from bonus funds before the percentage.
Do online casinos in Africa accept local payment methods?
The good ones are built around them. M-Pesa in Kenya and Tanzania, MTN MoMo in Ghana and Uganda, Paystack and OPay in Nigeria, Ozow and vouchers in South Africa. An operator that only takes cards is not serious about the market. Note that withdrawals normally return to the method you deposited with.
Can I play casino games in Africa on mobile?
Mobile is the primary way people play across the continent, not the fallback. The operators worth using build for low bandwidth — betPawa and MSport both do this deliberately. A heavy app that stalls on a weak connection is a worse product here regardless of how many games it carries.
Are live dealer games available at online casinos in Africa?
Yes, at most licensed operators, streamed from international studios. Roulette and blackjack tables are standard. They are the most data-hungry thing on any casino site, so on a metered connection they will cost considerably more than slots.
