# Cheltenham October Meeting 2026: Friday Horse Racing Tips, Best Bets & Race-by-Race Preview

The wait is finally over! Cheltenham is back, and the October Meeting means we can finally start getting properly stuck into some decent National Hunt racing again.

Friday's seven-race card looks competitive, with a mixture of exciting young horses, established handicappers and a few familiar faces returning for another campaign.

Now, one thing I always find interesting about these early-season meetings is trying to work out which horses are actually ready to go.

Some trainers will have their horses absolutely spot on for their first run, while others will be using Cheltenham as a stepping stone towards bigger targets later in the season. You've also got plenty of horses returning after successful campaigns who are still sitting right at the top of their handicap marks.

That's where I think we can potentially find a bit of value.

I've gone through all seven races and picked out the horses I like, including a couple at bigger prices where I think the race could be run to suit.

My Cheltenham NAP today is Cooltobecareless in the 15:00, while Fairlawn Skipper in the 16:45 is my Next Best.

Anyway, let's get stuck in!

## 13:15 Cheltenham

### SUNNY SOUTH WEST – 4/1 (Bet365)

I'm going to take a bit of a chance in the opener with hurdling newcomer Sunny South West for the Edward Power yard.

Now, there are plenty in here who could potentially need the run, and a few others who might just be struggling off their current handicap marks. So rather than siding with one of the more established horses, I'm going to take a chance on something that could have a fair bit of potential.

Sunny South West absolutely bolted up over 2 miles plus on the flat last time out, and looking at his pedigree, there's every reason to think he could develop into a pretty smart hurdler.

There's also a really nice piece of form going back to Galway in July.

He finished behind Abbotsford, who has since gone in again off a flat mark of 96. That alone is decent form, but what really caught my eye was that Sunny South West finished ahead of Willie Mullins' Daddy Long Legs.

Now, Daddy Long Legs has just absolutely destroyed the field by 7 lengths on his chase debut, so there's clearly a bit of substance to that Galway race.

Of course, flat form and jumping ability are two completely different things, and making your hurdling debut at Cheltenham isn't exactly the easiest introduction!

But if he takes to his hurdles and jumps reasonably well, I think he's got enough ability to go very close.

The danger for me at a bigger price is Handlethekettle at 14/1 for the RC Racing Club. He comes here fit, which could be a massive advantage at this time of year, and if he handles Cheltenham, I wouldn't be surprised to see him run a nice race.

But I'm happy to take the chance on Sunny South West. At 4/1, I think there's enough in his flat form and potential over hurdles to justify a bet.

Selection: SUNNY SOUTH WEST – WIN 4/1 Bet365

## 13:50 Cheltenham

### ICE JET – 4/1 (Paddy Power)

I'm going to take on the odds-on favourite Bowens On Fire here with Dan Skelton's big grey, Ice Jet.

Now, I was really impressed with Ice Jet last time out, particularly the way he went off in front and attacked his fences.

There's just something about this horse that makes me think Cheltenham could really suit him.

He's a bold jumper, he attacks his fences with confidence, and I like the way he gets into a rhythm when allowed to get on with things.

Looking at the make-up of this race, I think the early pace is going to be absolutely crucial.

Passing Diamond is another who could potentially want the lead, and the last thing we need is those two getting involved in a battle from the start and cutting each other's throats!

But if Ice Jet can sit prominently, find a nice rhythm and avoid doing too much too early, I genuinely think he could run a massive race.

And here's another thing.

When you've got a horse jumping boldly in front around Cheltenham, it can put pressure on those in behind. If Ice Jet gets into a lovely rhythm and attacks his fences, he might just force the favourite into making a mistake or two.

Of course, Bowens On Fire is odds-on for a reason, and he's clearly the one to beat.

But at 4/1, I think Ice Jet is the value play.

He looks the type who could absolutely love Cheltenham, and if Harry Skelton can get the fractions right, he might be difficult to catch.

Selection: ICE JET – WIN 4/1 Paddy Power

## 14:25 Cheltenham

### WONDERING WHY – 11/2 Each Way (4 Places)

Now, this is a horse I find really interesting, and I think Ben Pauling could have one here who is potentially very well handicapped.

Wondering Why won really nicely when last seen, making his handicap debut in a 0-120, and what impressed me was the way he was pulling away from the field towards the finish.

Nap Hand finished second that day, with Seraphic back in third.

And when you look at what those two have done since, the form starts to look pretty decent indeed!

Nap Hand came out and won on his latest start in October off a mark of 117, while Seraphic also managed to get his head in front last time out.

So the second and third have both come out and won since. That's exactly the type of form I like to see when going back through a horse's previous runs.

But here's where it gets really interesting.

Tomorrow's race is restricted to conditional jockeys, and Elliott England takes 8lb off Wondering Why's back.

That effectively puts him in here off a mark of 111, which is 4lb lower than the mark he won off last time!

Now, considering how well that form has worked out, I think there's every possibility this horse is still ahead of the handicapper.

The obvious question is fitness, as he hasn't been seen for a while.

But Ben Pauling is more than capable of getting a horse ready first time out, and I wouldn't be overly concerned about the break.

Of course, Cheltenham is a different test, but with the strength of that previous form and the weight he's effectively carrying, I think he could run a really big race.

At 11/2 with 4 places available, I'm more than happy to play each way.

Selection: WONDERING WHY – EACH WAY 11/2 (4 Places)

## 15:00 Cheltenham

### COOLTOBECARELESS – 7/4 (All Firms)

VIP NAP / Best Bet of the Day

Now, I don't normally like taking short prices in competitive races at Cheltenham, but I think I'm going to have to side with the favourite here.

The Skeltons look to have another really nice young horse on their hands in Cooltobecareless, and I was seriously impressed with what he did on his reappearance two weeks ago.

He absolutely demolished the field, winning by 13 lengths, and to my eye, there was still plenty left in the tank.

He travelled beautifully, jumped really well and went about his business like a horse who knew exactly what he was doing.

What I particularly liked was how straightforward he looked.

There was no messing around, no jumping left or right, no looking around when he hit the front. He just went about his job, ears pricked, and was never fully pushed out.

For a 6-year-old who still looks to be learning and improving, that's a pretty encouraging performance.

And the other thing is that this was his first run back.

You'd imagine he'll come on again for that outing, and if he does, then the opposition could have a serious problem here.

Of course, Cheltenham will present a different challenge, and he's going to need to jump just as fluently around this track.

But based on what I saw two weeks ago, I think he's got all the right attributes.

The 7/4 might not look particularly generous at first glance, especially when you compare it with some of the bigger prices we've got elsewhere on the card.

But sometimes you've just got to accept that the favourite is the most likely winner.

And for me, this is one of those occasions.

I think Cooltobecareless is a very straightforward horse with plenty more improvement to come, and I'm happy to make him my NAP of the day.

Selection: COOLTOBECARELESS – WIN 7/4 (All Firms)

## 15:35 Cheltenham

### SANS BRUIT – 9/1 Each Way (Bet365)

This is a really, really tough race, and I wouldn't be surprised if we saw a few different opinions on the winner.

Calico has won this race for the last two years, which is some achievement, and although he isn't getting any younger, you certainly can't dismiss a horse with that sort of record around Cheltenham.

I'm also a huge fan of JPR One, and Joe Tizzard's yard is absolutely flying at the moment.

So there are plenty of reasons to respect the opposition.

But at the prices, I'm going to take a bit of a chance on Paul Nicholls' Sans Bruit.

Now, this is very much a race-shape selection for me.

Looking through the field, there doesn't appear to be a great deal of obvious early pace, and I'd absolutely love to see Harry Cobden get Sans Bruit out in front and try to dictate things.

If he can jump off, get into a nice rhythm and control the fractions, I think he could be a very difficult horse to pass.

There's nothing worse than backing a front-runner only to see three or four others taking him on for the lead, but I don't think that's necessarily going to be the case here.

And that's what makes the 9/1 interesting.

We're not necessarily backing the horse with the strongest recent form in the race. We're backing a horse who could get the perfect tactical setup.

If Cobden manages to get him into a lovely rhythm and dictate from the front, we could find ourselves with a 9/1 chance turning for home who has plenty of the opposition in trouble.

Of course, it's not guaranteed he'll get an uncontested lead, and if they go too quickly, that could completely change things.

But that's the gamble I'm prepared to take.

At 9/1, I think Sans Bruit is a really interesting each-way play.

Selection: SANS BRUIT – EACH WAY 9/1 Bet365

## 16:10 Cheltenham

At this time of year, that's always something I like to see.

There's no guessing whether he's going to need the run or whether he'll be blowing up turning for home. He should arrive here fully tuned up and ready to go.

Now, obviously we're taking a short price at 10/11, and you know me, I generally prefer finding something a bit bigger.

But sometimes you can spend far too long trying to get an odds-on favourite beaten when the obvious answer is staring you in the face!

On what we've seen, I think Mr Hollywood is the standout horse in this race.

If he reproduces anything like his best form, he's going to be very difficult to beat.

Selection: MR HOLLYWOOD – WIN 10/11 William Hill

## 16:45 Cheltenham

### FAIRLAWN SKIPPER – 4/1

NEXT BEST BET

The final race of the day, and I'm going to take a chance on Mickey Bowen's Fairlawn Skipper.

Now, Little Legend is clearly the horse everyone has to beat here.

He's absolutely flying up the handicap, bidding for the hat-trick, and Olly Murphy looks to have found another nice opportunity for him.

But there comes a point where the handicapper starts to make life difficult, and I just wonder whether this might be the race where he finds one a little too good.

Fairlawn Skipper is the horse I want to be with.

I really liked his victory last time out over 2m5f, particularly the way he responded when the pressure was applied.

He travelled nicely, but what impressed me most was how he knuckled down when push came to shove.

Rather than finding very little when asked, he actually started pulling away from the opposition towards the finish.

That's always something I like to see in a staying horse.

And looking at the way he finished that race, I don't think the step up in trip here is going to be any problem whatsoever.

In fact, there's every chance it could bring out a little more improvement.

The form of his latest win has also worked out nicely.

Limerick Leader gave him a proper race that day and has since come out and won well, which gives that performance a bit more substance.

I'm also a big fan of Mickey Bowen, particularly when it comes to staying races.

He knows how to prepare these types of horses, and I wouldn't be surprised if Fairlawn Skipper has been targeted at this race.

Of course, Little Legend is going to take some beating if he continues improving, but at the prices, I think Fairlawn Skipper represents the better bet.

He's progressive, his recent form has been franked, and I think there's every chance the extra distance brings out another improvement.

At 4/1, I'm happy to have a win bet and make him my Next Best of the day.

Selection: FAIRLAWN SKIPPER – WIN 4/1
